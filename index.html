Our process

Four simple steps to moving day.

From your first quote to the last box, here is exactly what to expect when you move with Bayou City Movers.

Step 01 of 04

Get your free quote

  • Answer a few quick questions about your move
  • Choose an instant quote, video walkthrough, or in home estimate
  • Hear back from a specialist within 1 business hour

Takes under 60 seconds

Step 02 of 04

Plan your move day

  • Crew size and truck matched to your home
  • Packing supplies delivered if you need them
  • Parking and elevator permits handled for you

Ideally 2 to 3 weeks before moving day

Step 03 of 04

Pack and move with care

  • Furniture padded and shrink wrapped
  • Boxes labeled room by room
  • Specialty items crated and secured

Moving day, starting as early as 7am

Step 04 of 04

Settle into your new home

  • Furniture placed exactly where you want it
  • Beds and tables reassembled
  • Empty boxes and packing waste hauled away

Same day, before the crew heads out
